## Design Context

### Users
Cua serves AI-agent developers, research teams, and infrastructure operators who need to run, evaluate, and train computer-use agents across Linux, Windows, macOS, and Android. Their primary job is understanding the fleet, driver, benchmark, and data products quickly enough to choose a local, cloud, or managed path.

### Brand Personality
Technical, cinematic, and assured. The experience should feel like a precise research instrument with an editorial point of view: quiet rather than flashy, highly legible, and credible to expert developers.

### Aesthetic Direction
Faithfully reproduce cua.ai's dark editorial system: a pure-black canvas, Instrument Serif display typography, Urbanist body copy, JetBrains Mono micro-labels, pale-blue accents, atmospheric cool-gray light, fine dividers, large vertical intervals, restrained charcoal panels, and detailed operating-system scenes. Avoid purple AI gradients, glassmorphism, generic SaaS card grids, heavy shadows, and crowded first-view UI.

### Design Principles
1. Let typography and negative space carry the hierarchy.
2. Use blue only for active emphasis, primary actions, and small technical details.
3. Keep component geometry flat and restrained: subtle borders, modest radii, no nested card stacks.
4. Preserve all core functionality on mobile by reflowing navigation, media scenes, FAQs, and CTAs for touch.
5. Use motion for state and narrative only: gentle entrance reveals, cycling hero language, dropdown/accordion transitions, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
